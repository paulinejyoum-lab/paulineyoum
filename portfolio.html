Wireframe Chair

Modeled on Nomad Sculpt, then vertically and horizantally sliced on Fusion. Slice shapes traced into illustrator file and CNC cut into plywood. Plywood used as a mold for steel rods to be bent on its perimeters. Steel welded into place and supports added inside to be able to become a fully standing and able to be sat on with full force chair.
